Real frames · neoui snapshot tests

What the phone shows

Every screen here is a real frame from neoui, the Rust UI. The snapshot tests render it with the same software renderer that runs on the Pixel, at the phone's 1080 × 2160. The names, events and networks in them are demo data.

Source: NeoSymbian/neoui · snapshots/

Home and the shell

The home screen with its clock and widgets, the quick settings shade, and the app switcher.

Calls and people

Calls go out over LTE with two-way audio on the phone. These frames show the call view, the call log, Contacts and Messages.

Settings, Wi-Fi and tools

Wi-Fi joins WPA2 networks on 2.4 and 5 GHz. The Terminal runs the 2010 text programs through a console server written in Rust.

A system keyboard in three languages

English, Armenian (phonetic) and Russian. The globe key switches layouts, and any text field raises the keyboard.
